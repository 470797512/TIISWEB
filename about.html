Welcome to TIIS

For Today, For Tomorrow

The Institute of International Studies is a private higher education provider based in Sydney, with a second campus in Melbourne. We are registered with the Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency as provider PRV14085, and with CRICOS as provider 03705J to teach international students.

We are deliberately small. Courses are built around what employers in accounting, business and IT actually ask for, and reviewed with our academic board rather than rewritten each year for marketing.

Our Commitment

Industry-Relevant Education

Where a professional body accredits a qualification, we seek that accreditation rather than claiming equivalence.

Graduates of our Master of Professional Accounting program become eligible for accreditation with CPA Australia, Chartered Accountants Australian and New Zealand (CAANZ), and ACCA. These strong ties ensure you enter the global job market well-prepared.
