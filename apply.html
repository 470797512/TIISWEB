Admissions

How to Apply

Applications are made by email. There is no application fee and no online portal.

Application Process

The Four Steps

Most applicants hear back within a few working days.

1

Gather Your Documentation

Academic transcripts, identification, and evidence of English proficiency. What counts depends on the course level.

2

Submit Your Application

Email the form and your documents to info@tiis.edu.au.

3

Receive Your Offer

If you meet the entry criteria, we issue a Letter of Offer setting out your fees and start date.

4

Pay & Enrol

Pay the fees in your offer and we issue a Confirmation of Enrolment. International students need this for a student visa.

Entry Requirements

Admission Criteria

Requirements differ between undergraduate and postgraduate study. If you are close but not certain you qualify, ask us before applying.

Undergraduate Programs

  • • Completion of Australian Year 12 or equivalent international qualification
  • • English language proficiency (IELTS 6.0 or equivalent)
  • • Additional requirements as specified per course

Postgraduate Programs

  • • A recognised bachelor's degree or equivalent
  • • English language proficiency (IELTS 6.5 or equivalent)
  • • Relevant work experience may be considered for certain programs

Payment Information

Fees are due before each study period starts. Payment can be arranged by instalment.

Upon fee payment, you will receive a Confirmation of Enrolment (COE), which is required for your visa application (international students).

For detailed fee information for specific courses, please refer to our fee schedule or contact our admissions team.
