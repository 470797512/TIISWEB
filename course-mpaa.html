Course Overview

Professional Accounting Program Overview

The MPAA at TIIS develops advanced accounting knowledge, analytical capability, and professional expertise for dynamic global business environments. The program integrates core accounting disciplines, advanced professional accounting units, and strategic leadership capability.

✓Apply advanced accounting knowledge in complex organisational contexts
✓Analyse, interpret, and evaluate financial and business data
✓Demonstrate leadership, communication, and professional judgement
✓Critically evaluate contemporary issues impacting the accounting profession

A professional-accounting pathway

  1. Begin with the GCPA

    Four foundation subjects are shared with the MPAA.

  2. Continue your qualification

    With admission and four credits approved, complete the remaining 12 prescribed subjects.

  3. Explore professional recognition

    CPA Australia, CA ANZ and ACCA accreditation addresses academic requirements; membership and designation have further conditions.

Progression is subject to admission and credit approval. With all four certificate subjects credited, 12 further subjects complete the 16-subject master’s degree; the certificate is not an additional four subjects.
