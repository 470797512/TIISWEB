A pathway through the GCBA

  1. Begin with four subjects

    The Graduate Certificate in Business Administration covers MBA601, MBA602, MPA503 and MBA603: four core subjects, 24 credits, over 16 weeks.

  2. Progress to the MBA

    Apply for progression and credit. These subjects align with the initial blocks of the MBA; admission and credit approval apply.

  3. Complete the remaining study

    With all four subjects credited, complete nine further core subjects, including the capstone, and three electives.

Admissions

Entry requirements

Academic entry

An undergraduate degree, or a relevant graduate certificate or diploma, from a recognised Australian institution or an equivalent overseas institution.

English proficiency

IELTS 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0, or PTE 58 overall with no score below 50.

Other evidence may be accepted, including an undergraduate degree taught in English or Australian Year 12 with a pass in General English within the past two years, subject to assessment.

Alternative admission

Applicants without the standard qualifications may be considered through special admission arrangements. Assessment may include a portfolio of academic and professional experience and an interview. Relevant employment evidence must cover at least three years in the past five years.

GCBA entry pathway

If you do not meet direct MBA entry requirements, you may be eligible to begin with the Graduate Certificate in Business Administration and progress with approved credit.
