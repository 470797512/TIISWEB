About TIIS

A Small Provider,
Accredited Since 2015

The Institute of International Studies is an Australian higher education provider registered with TEQSA. We teach business, accounting and information technology to domestic and international students at two city campuses.

Our Master of Professional Accounting is accredited by CPA Australia, Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand, and ACCA. Class sizes are small, and the people who teach you are the people you see on campus.

Practical Details

What You Should Know
Before Applying

The things prospective students ask us about most often, in one place.

Accreditation

Registered with TEQSA as provider PRV14085. The Master of Professional Accounting is accredited by CPA Australia, CAANZ and ACCA.

Six intakes a year

January, March, May, July, September and November, so you rarely wait more than two months to start.

How you study

Most courses run face-to-face, online or blended. Delivery depends on the course and your visa conditions.

Where you study

Sydney and Melbourne, except the Graduate Certificate in Professional Accounting, which runs in Sydney only.

Admissions

How to Apply

Applications go to admissions by email. There is no online portal and no application fee.

1

Gather Documents

Academic transcripts, ID, and evidence of English proficiency. Requirements differ by course level.

2

Submit Application

Email the completed form and your documents to info@tiis.edu.au.

3

Receive Offer

If you meet the entry criteria, we issue a Letter of Offer for your course.

4

Pay & Enrol

Pay the fees in your offer. We then issue a Confirmation of Enrolment, which international students need for a visa.
