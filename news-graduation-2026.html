TIIS Graduation Ceremony 2026: Celebrating Our Sydney and Melbourne Graduands

Recognising the achievements, dedication and next chapter of the Class of 2026.

The Institute of International Studies

The Institute of International Studies (TIIS) is celebrating the Class of 2026 with graduation ceremonies in Sydney and Melbourne.

The graduation ceremony marks an important milestone in the academic journey of our graduands and provides an opportunity to formally recognise their achievements, dedication and successful completion of their studies. TIIS celebrates this occasion together with graduands, their families, guests and members of the wider TIIS community.

Sydney Graduation Ceremony

Friday, 30 October 2026Sydney Masonic CentreSydney, NSW

Melbourne Graduation Ceremony

Friday, 20 November 2026The Wheeler CentreMelbourne, VIC

Information for graduands

Further information regarding the graduation ceremony has been sent directly by email to eligible graduands. These communications include important details relating to RSVP requirements, ceremony arrangements, gown collection, guest attendance and other relevant information.

Eligible graduands are encouraged to review the email carefully and complete any required actions within the advised timeframe.

TIIS is proud to recognise the accomplishments of the Class of 2026 and to celebrate this significant occasion with our Sydney and Melbourne graduands.
