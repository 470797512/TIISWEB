Your formal offer

What Confirms Your Fees

Your Letter of Offer sets out your course, commencement date, tuition amount and payment requirements. Read it carefully before accepting and paying.

  • Fees are payable according to the signed Letter of Offer and any approved payment arrangement.
  • Later study periods may be charged at the fee current at that time, as set out in the applicable policy and enrolment documents.
  • International students should also plan for Overseas Student Health Cover, visa costs, travel and living expenses.
Payment responsibilities

Due Dates & Enrolment

Pay by the due dates stated in your offer or invoice. If you are unable to make a scheduled payment, contact Student Services before the due date so the available options can be discussed.

Do not rely on an estimate

The downloadable schedule is general information. Your Letter of Offer and TIIS invoice are the definitive records for your individual enrolment.

Changes and withdrawals

Refund Requests

Refund outcomes depend on why and when an enrolment changes. Review the Refund Policy before withdrawing, and contact Student Services for the current application process and required evidence.

Submitting a request does not itself guarantee a refund. TIIS assesses each request under the policy and the terms of the student agreement.
