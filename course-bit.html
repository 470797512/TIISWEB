Course Overview

Information Technology Degree Overview

The Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) at TIIS develops broad and coherent knowledge and practical skills in ICT. You'll gain expertise across software development, networking, web and mobile technologies, and cyber security.

Complete 17 compulsory subjects and select seven electives from one or both streams: Web and Mobile Applications Development and Cyber Security.

✓Demonstrate broad knowledge of IT systems and technologies
✓Design, develop, and deploy software and IT solutions
✓Apply cybersecurity concepts and ethical frameworks in IT practice
✓Work independently and collaboratively in professional contexts

Build your IT study plan

  1. Develop the foundation

    Complete the 17-subject core alongside your approved electives.

  2. Select seven electives

    Choose from one or both streams; confirm prerequisites and availability.

  3. Apply your learning

    Complete BIT303 within the core. Further study may include the MCS, subject to admission.

The two pools are alternatives to choose from, not 14 additional compulsory subjects. This is a course-composition guide, not a semester timetable.
