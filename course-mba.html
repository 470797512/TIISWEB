Course Overview

MBA Program Overview

The MBA at TIIS is a postgraduate qualification designed to develop advanced business knowledge and leadership capabilities for a global business environment. It combines 13 compulsory subjects, including the Business Capstone Project, with three electives. Choose a Marketing & Entrepreneurship focus, a Global Business focus, or a mix of electives.

✓Apply advanced business knowledge in complex organisational contexts
✓Analyse and solve organisational problems with strategic thinking
✓Demonstrate leadership and ethical decision-making
✓Communicate effectively with diverse stakeholders

A graduate-certificate pathway

  1. Start with the GCBA

    Four business subjects also form part of the MBA core.

  2. Continue into the MBA

    Apply for admission and recognition of the four completed subjects.

  3. Complete the remaining 12

    With four credits approved, finish nine further core subjects and three electives.

Progression is subject to admission and credit approval. With all four certificate subjects credited, 12 further subjects complete the 16-subject master’s degree; the certificate is not an additional four subjects.
