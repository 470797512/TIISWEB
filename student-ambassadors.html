Student Ambassadors help other students feel informed, welcome and connected to the TIIS community.

The program is an extra-curricular opportunity for domestic and international on-campus students. Ambassadors represent TIIS to current, new and prospective students, as well as external stakeholders.

Who can take part?

Current on-campus students

Expressions of interest are open to eligible domestic and international TIIS students. Selection and activity schedules depend on current program needs.

Ambassador Responsibilities

Welcome Students at Orientation

Help new students find their way, understand the day and feel comfortable joining the TIIS community.

Support the First Weeks of Study

Share practical peer guidance with first-year students during the early transition to higher education.

Share Your Student Experience

Offer prospective and new students a genuine account of studying and participating in campus life at TIIS.

Help Deliver Events

Contribute to planning and running activities that support academic transition, community and employability.

Represent TIIS Responsibly

Take part in approved communication and outreach activities while representing TIIS values and culture accurately.

Balancing commitments

Study Comes First

Ambassador activities are planned around academic commitments. Students are not expected to miss classes, and required training is scheduled outside class time.

Some events and workshops may take place during TIIS holidays or weekends. Expectations are kept reasonable around assessment and examination periods.
