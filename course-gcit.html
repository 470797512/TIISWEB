Course Overview

Graduate Certificate Overview

The GCIT provides essential IT knowledge and practical technical skills across core areas of Information Technology. Suitable for graduates without a computing background, professionals transitioning into IT, or those aiming to pathway into postgraduate IT programs.

✓Apply foundational IT knowledge across programming, networking and cyber security
✓Analyse and solve technical problems using IT tools and methods
✓Design and develop basic IT solutions for business environments
✓Pathway into the Master of Cyber Security with credit transfer

Graduate with a certificate — or continue

  1. Complete four subjects

    Finish the subjects listed above and meet the certificate’s award requirements.

  2. Choose your next step

    Finish with the certificate, or apply to the MCS and request credit.

  3. Build toward a master’s

    With four subjects credited, complete 12 further subjects for the 16-subject master’s award.

Progression is subject to admission and credit approval. With all four certificate subjects credited, 12 further subjects complete the 16-subject master’s degree; the certificate is not an additional four subjects.
