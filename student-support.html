How we can help

Support Areas

Start with Student Services if you are unsure which team or service you need.

01

Academic Support

Individual academic guidance, study and assessment workshops, referencing support and access to the Learning Centre.

02

Personal Wellbeing & Counselling

General support with homesickness, stress, motivation, conflict or personal concerns, with referral to professional counselling when needed.

03

Orientation

A compulsory introduction to academic expectations, Moodle, learning resources, IT facilities, safety and student services.

04

English Language Support

Learning-needs assessment and English for Academic Purposes support for students who need additional language assistance.

05

Accommodation Guidance

Practical guidance on searching for housing, reviewing agreements, understanding costs and identifying reputable sources of information.

06

Financial Hardship

Information about the evidence and application process where unforeseen circumstances make an agreed tuition payment difficult.

07

Safety & Health

Campus safety guidance, incident reporting and referral to health, emergency or community services when specialist help is required.

08

Careers & Employment

Résumé and interview preparation, employability activities and authoritative information about workplace rights in Australia.
