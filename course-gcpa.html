Course Overview

Graduate Certificate Overview

The GCPA provides foundational accounting knowledge and practical financial skills for entry-level professional practice. It is designed as a standalone qualification or as a structured pathway into the Master of Professional Accounting (Advanced) (MPAA).

✓Demonstrate understanding of core accounting, legal, and financial principles
✓Analyse financial data and apply accounting concepts to business problems
✓Interpret and communicate financial information effectively
✓Pathway directly into the MPAA with credit subject to admission and approval

Graduate with a certificate — or continue

  1. Complete four subjects

    Finish the subjects listed above and meet the certificate’s award requirements.

  2. Choose your next step

    Finish with the certificate, or apply to the MPAA and request credit.

  3. Build toward a master’s

    With four subjects credited, complete 12 further subjects for the 16-subject master’s award.

Progression is subject to admission and credit approval. With all four certificate subjects credited, 12 further subjects complete the 16-subject master’s degree; the certificate is not an additional four subjects.
